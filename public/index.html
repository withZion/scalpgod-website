Questions

If yours isn't here, ask when we message you from the waitlist.

How are the two strategies different?

Key Levels waits for price to break an important level with the trend, then takes the first clean pullback to it, on all 20 pairs. Quiet market + AI looks for calm, steady trends on BTC and ETH, and an AI model lets through only the setups it rates highest.

Why aren't all 27 board pairs here?

Some have stops so tight that fees and spread would eat them, BNB and TRX among them. The rest aren't on a live strategy yet. Members will hear when a pair is added.

Does it place trades for me?

No. It sends a plan and nothing else. It never asks for account access and holds no keys that could trade or withdraw.

What if I see a signal late?

Check the price and the time limit before you act. If price has already moved well past the entry, skip that one and wait for the next.

Is this financial advice?

No. Each signal is a trade idea, and every message says so. Scalping is risky, and you can lose money on any trade.
