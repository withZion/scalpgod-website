Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

ScalpGOD is run by Zion Ojuwonlo. This page covers the waitlist on this website, which is the only thing that collects your details right now. When paid access opens, this page will be updated before anything new is collected.

What we collect

  • Your Telegram username. You type it into the waitlist form.
  • Your email address, if you choose to give one.
  • The time you joined and the country Cloudflare says your connection came from. We never record your city or exact location.

To stop spam, the site also counts sign-up attempts from each IP address. That count deletes itself after one hour and isn't stored with your sign-up.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to message you when access opens and to reply if you write to us. We don't sell your details or share them for advertising, and we don't add you to any other list.

Where it's kept

Sign-ups are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Cloudflare also keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads. This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or tracking.

How long we keep it

Until access opens and we've told you, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Email zion.ojuwonlo@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, or tell us your Telegram username. We'll act on it within 7 days. If you're in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 also gives you these rights.
